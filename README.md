# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing e-commerce sales,
profit, customers, marketing channels, and operational performance.

The project focuses on transforming raw business data into
interactive dashboards and actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Analyze customer and regional performance
- Understand sales trends over time
- Identify profitable and low-performing areas
- Support data-driven business decision-making

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Tableau
- SQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## 📊 Dashboard

The interactive Tableau dashboard provides insights into:

- Sales Performance
- Profitability
- Customer Performance
- Product Performance
- Marketing Channels
- Operational Performance

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### Sales Analysis
Analyze sales performance across products, categories,
regions, and time periods.

### Profit Analysis
Identify profitable and low-performing areas and understand
factors affecting overall profitability.

### Customer Analysis
Evaluate customer behavior and contribution to business
performance.

### Operational Analysis
Analyze operational metrics to identify opportunities for
improving business performance.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- High-performing products and categories
- Important customer segments
- Regional performance differences
- Sales and profit trends
- Areas requiring business attention
